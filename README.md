## Summary of Project
Color blindness affects around 300 million people globally (1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women), posing challenges in daily life, from reading traffic signals to interpreting workplace data. Many individuals remain unaware of their condition due to limited access to affordable and accurate testing.

**ColorVision** is a user-friendly mobile app that addresses this gap by providing accessible, clinically recognized color blindness tests, such as the Ishihara and Farnsworth D-15 tests. Users can diagnose their color vision deficiency from the comfort of their home. The app not only identifies the condition but also educates users about different types of color blindness (e.g., red-green and blue-yellow deficiencies) and their daily implications.

In addition to benefiting individual users, ColorVision supports educators and parents by helping to identify and accommodate individuals with color vision challenges. The app’s design prioritizes accessibility, making it suitable for users of all ages and technical skill levels.

Compared to competitors, ColorVision stands out by offering a comprehensive set of tools, including multiple testing options and educational resources, all in one affordable, easy-to-use app. It empowers users with knowledge while promoting broader awareness of color vision deficiencies to reduce stigma and foster inclusivity.

By enhancing accessibility and raising awareness, **ColorVision** aims to improve the quality of life for millions and foster a more inclusive society.

## Project Analysis

### Value Proposition
ColorVision addresses the challenge of diagnosing color blindness, a condition affecting approximately 300 million people worldwide. Many individuals remain undiagnosed due to limited access to affordable, accurate testing, leading to difficulties in interpreting color-coded information, reading traffic signals, and performing tasks in color-dependent professions.

ColorVision provides an affordable, easy-to-use app with clinically validated color blindness tests, enabling users to test at home and gain insights into their condition. This empowers individuals to take control of their condition, enhancing daily functionality and promoting inclusivity. The app also assists educators and employers in identifying and supporting individuals with color vision challenges, leading to better accommodations in learning and work environments.

### Primary Purpose
The primary purpose of ColorVision is to deliver an efficient platform for testing color blindness and gaining insights into color vision. The app aims to make testing accessible and affordable, empowering users with accurate results and educational resources. By focusing on ease of use, ColorVision aims to provide a tool that supports individuals, educators, parents, and employers in fostering inclusivity.

### Target Audience
ColorVision primarily targets individuals between 18 to 40 who suspect they have color vision deficiencies. Additionally, the app is designed for students, professionals in color-critical fields, and individuals who face challenges in day-to-day activities involving color differentiation.

Secondary audiences include parents, educators, and employers who want to assess and support those with color vision deficiencies. Digital marketing strategies, social media platforms, and collaborations with educational and professional institutions will help reach these audiences.

### Success Criteria
Success will be measured by the following key factors:

1. **User Adoption:** Track app downloads and active users to gauge reach and popularity.
2. **User Feedback:** Monitor positive ratings, reviews, and direct feedback for app functionality and ease of use.
3. **Test Completion Rate:** Measure engagement by tracking the percentage of users completing the color blindness test.
4. **Financial Performance:** Assess revenue from in-app purchases and premium feature subscriptions.
5. **User Retention:** High retention rates will indicate sustained value provided to users.

## Competitor Analysis
ColorVision competes with options like EnChroma, various color blindness test apps, and traditional in-office testing. Unlike competitors, ColorVision offers affordable, clinically validated testing with comprehensive educational resources. This approach aims to fill the accessibility and inclusivity gaps in existing solutions.

## Monetization Model
ColorVision will use a freemium model with the following strategies:

1. **Limited Free Tests:** Users get a limited number of free tests; further tests require payment or a subscription for unlimited access.
2. **Advertisements:** The free version includes ads shown before test results, generating revenue from non-paying users.
3. **Future Premium Features:** Additional features with enhanced functionality will be introduced as premium options.

## Initial Design

### Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
The MVP for ColorVision will include:

- **User-Friendly Interface:** Simple, intuitive design for users of all ages.
- **Color Blindness Tests:** Ishihara and Farnsworth D-15 tests with immediate feedback.
- **Results Summary:** Clear test results with explanations and possible next steps.
- **Educational Resources:** Informative content on color blindness types and management tips.
- **Accessibility Features:** Adjustable text size and settings for user accessibility.

### Scope and Limitations
The MVP will not initially include advanced features like personalized progress tracking or healthcare integration. Multilingual support will be added in future updates. The focus is on essential testing and educational functionalities to validate the app’s core concept.

---

## UI/UX Design
The UI/UX design focuses on simplicity, with core components as follows:

1. **Home Screen:** Clear buttons for starting tests, viewing history, and accessing information.
2. **Test Process:** Straightforward test instructions and progress indicators.
3. **Results Screen:** Clear format for results with text and visual highlights.
4. **Basic Navigation:** Familiar mobile navigation like a bottom menu bar.
5. **Accessibility Options:** Adjustable font sizes and high-contrast options for users with visual impairments.

---

## Technical Architecture
The technical architecture for ColorVision includes:

1. **Mobile Application Framework:** Using React Native or Flutter for cross-platform compatibility.
2. **Data Storage:** Local storage for preferences and test history, with cloud storage for advanced features.
3. **APIs and Third-Party Services:** Integrate Google AdMob for advertisements and Firebase for basic cloud storage and user authentication.
4. **Backend Server:** Initial backend with Node.js for user authentication and storage management.
5. **Security and Privacy:** Basic encryption for data protection, with ongoing security enhancements.

Success will be measured by system stability, low crash rates, user retention, and smooth integration with third-party services.

## Challenges and Open Questions

### Technical Challenges
- **Form Factor:** Ensuring accurate test display across different devices with varying screen sizes and resolutions.
  - *Solution:* Use responsive design to maintain test accuracy on all devices.

- **Data Restoration on Reinstall:** Users may lose test results and settings upon reinstall.
  - *Solution:* Implement cloud storage or local backups for automatic restoration.

- **Resource Constraints (Internet and Storage):** Optimizing offline functionality for users with limited internet and storage.
  - *Solution:* Lightweight app design with offline test and result access.

### Open Questions
- Which additional features would enhance user experience?
- How can we effectively market ColorVision to reach target users, especially those unaware of their color vision deficiencies?

By addressing these challenges and questions, ColorVision aims to proactively improve user experience and gather insights to inform further development.
